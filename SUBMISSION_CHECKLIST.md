# Trainee submission checklist

## Prepared for you

- Complete Java/XML source, including all Morning Brew stretch goals.
- Meaningful Git history with more than 8 commits and a documented starter.
- README with clone, build and run instructions.
- Buildable, debug-signed APK, screen recording and verification report.

## What only you can supply

1. Submit your GitHub repository link, APK and demo to the trainer using
   their submission channel. No trainer address or submission portal was given.
2. Review the acceptance checklist and choose your own provisional grade.
   Your trainer determines the final grade; runtime evidence is in VERIFICATION.md.
3. Write your honest baseline reflections:
   - One thing that was harder than you expected: __________________
   - One thing you would do differently next time: ________________
4. Tell the trainer that a standalone starter was used because their original
   starter was not provided, and that you requested all stretch features and
   saved state. Ask whether they also want your work applied to their starter.
5. GitHub publication is complete: https://github.com/TalentFlowAi2026/Morning-Brew
   Publish-to-GitHub.bat is available for later updates.

The training instruction to ask for help within 30 minutes and your personal
learning reflections are process/learning criteria, not app features. They
cannot be retroactively verified or written as personal claims on your behalf.
